Spider Men

Spider-Man, whose real name is Peter Parker, is a young superhero in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He gains his powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. Peter has superhuman strength, speed, agility, and reflexes. He can climb walls and uses his web-shooters to swing through the city and trap enemies. He also has a special Spider-Sense that warns him of danger. He becomes an ally of Iron Man and later fights alongside the Avengers against powerful enemies such as Thanos and his forces. Spider-Man is best known for his agility, Spider-Sense, web-slinging abilities, intelligence, and strong sense of responsibility.
