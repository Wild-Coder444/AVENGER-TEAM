Falcon

Falcon, whose real name is Sam Wilson, is a skilled superhero and former U.S. Air Force pararescueman in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He uses a specially designed mechanical wing suit that allows him to fly and maneuver through the air. Sam is highly trained in hand-to-hand combat, aerial combat, and tactical operations. He becomes a close ally of Captain America and later takes on the mantle of Captain America. He fights alongside the Avengers and other heroes against enemies such as Hydra forces and Thanos's army. Falcon is best known for his flight technology, combat skills, courage, and his connection with Captain America.
