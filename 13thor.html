Thor

Thor is the God of Thunder and one of the most powerful heroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is the son of Odin and the brother of Loki. Thor possesses superhuman strength, speed, durability, and the ability to control lightning and thunder. He is also a skilled warrior and uses powerful weapons such as Mjolnir and Stormbreaker. He becomes a key member of the Avengers and fights against powerful enemies such as Loki, Hela, Ultron, and Thanos. Thor is best known for his immense strength, lightning powers, warrior skills, and control over thunder and storms.
