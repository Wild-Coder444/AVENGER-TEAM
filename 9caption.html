Caption America

Captain America, whose real name is Steve Rogers, is a super-soldier and one of the most important heroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Before becoming Captain America, Steve was a physically weak but courageous young man who volunteered for the Super-Soldier Program. The experiment gives him enhanced strength, speed, agility, reflexes, and durability. He uses his famous vibranium shield as both a defensive and offensive weapon. Steve becomes a founding member and leader of the Avengers. He fights against enemies such as Red Skull, Hydra, Ultron, and Thanos's forces. Captain America is best known for his courage, leadership, super-soldier abilities, combat skills, and iconic shield.
