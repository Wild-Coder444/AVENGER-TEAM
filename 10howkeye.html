Hawkeye

Hawkeye, whose real name is Clint Barton, is a highly skilled marksman and one of the original members of the Avengers. Unlike many other Avengers, he does not have superhuman powers. Clint is an expert in archery, hand-to-hand combat, and tactical planning. He uses a specially designed bow with different types of arrows for different situations. He fights alongside the Avengers against enemies such as Loki, Ultron, and Thanos's forces. Hawkeye is best known for his incredible accuracy, archery skills, combat abilities, and tactical intelligence.
