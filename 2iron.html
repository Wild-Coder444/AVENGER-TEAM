Iron men

Iron Man, whose real name is Tony Stark, is a genius inventor, engineer, and billionaire in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He creates a powerful armored suit using advanced technology and an Arc Reactor as its energy source. The suit gives him abilities such as flight, enhanced strength, powerful weapons, and advanced protection. Tony Stark becomes one of the founding members of the Avengers and fights against many powerful enemies, including Iron Monger, Whiplash, Ultron, and Thanos. Throughout the movies, Tony changes from a selfish businessman into a responsible hero who is willing to sacrifice himself to protect others. Iron Man is best known for his intelligence, technology, courage, and role in the Avengers
