Vision

Vision is an advanced android and one of the powerful heroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is created using J.A.R.V.I.S., advanced technology, and the Mind Stone. Vision possesses superhuman strength, flight, durability, and energy projection. He can also become intangible, allowing him to pass through solid objects. He becomes a member of the Avengers and fights against enemies such as Ultron and Thanos's forces. Vision is best known for his advanced technology, Mind Stone, powerful energy abilities, flight, and ability to phase through objects
