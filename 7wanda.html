Scarlet Witch

Scarlet Witch, whose real name is Wanda Maximoff, is one of the most powerful heroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. She gains her extraordinary abilities after being exposed to the Mind Stone during experiments. Wanda can use chaos magic, telekinesis, energy manipulation, and mind manipulation. She can also create powerful energy fields and influence reality. She becomes an important member of the Avengers and fights against enemies such as Ultron and Thanos. Scarlet Witch is best known for her immense magical power, telekinesis, reality manipulation, and emotional strength.
