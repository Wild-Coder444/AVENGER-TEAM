Hulk

Hulk, whose real name is Bruce Banner, is a brilliant scientist in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. During an experiment involving gamma radiation, Bruce Banner is transformed into the Hulk. Hulk possesses incredible strength, durability, and healing ability. His physical power increases greatly when he becomes angry. Bruce later learns to control and combine his intelligence with Hulk's strength. He becomes a member of the Avengers and fights alongside heroes such as Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, and Black Widow. Hulk plays an important role in major battles against powerful enemies, including Ultron and Thanos. Hulk is best known for his incredible strength, durability, and the combination of Bruce Banner's intelligence with Hulk's power.
